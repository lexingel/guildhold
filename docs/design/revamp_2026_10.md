# Revamp, October 2026: fewer modes, deeper rifts, a living camp

Decided with the user on 2026-10-08. Rule for the whole revamp: **new texture,
no new rules.** The Paths rework (0.62) spent the player's learning budget on
hero raising; everything here either removes a rule or adds decisions that use
verbs the player already knows (fight, pick a route, spend Gold/Essence,
assign a hero).

Build order: Phase 1 (simplify) → Phase 2 (camp events) → Phase 3 (lane map).

## Phase 1: simplify (0.63)

### Endless: hidden, not deleted
- A flag (`GameData.ENDLESS_ENABLED := false`) hides the real-time Endless
  (SurvivorsRun/SurvivorsView, ~2.9k lines). Code and tests stay, so it can
  return as a postgame mode.
- The Rift Hall's middle gate opens **the Descent** directly (today it opens
  a choice between the Descent and Endless).
- Hidden with it: the Endless coach and guide entries, the `endless_five`
  achievement, the Standings column and the records stat. The Act II outro
  line that "opens the Endless Rift" is reworded to point at the Descent.
- Nothing else needs it: Act IV's "free a lost champion" and Nightblade's
  `freed` gate are met through the Descent and Rank B+ pillars. Champion
  levels still matter for Boon and Call in normal runs.

### Riftbreak: the tower defense goes, the breach stays
The breach is the campaign's pressure clock (why go to a rift today), and it
carries story, Act VI and two subclass gates. Only the defense minigame goes.
- **Breach rift:** defending a breach is a short rift of back-to-back
  fights at the breach's rank with no camp, shop or campfire between them;
  HP carries. 3 fights (combat, elite, a Breach Warden), the Act VI gate 4
  (with a boss). Win = held (today's `resolve_breach` payout); loss = today's
  losses (Essence/Gold above the bill, 1-2 damaged buildings, wounds).
- The countdown, the "all rifts blocked" rule, story hooks, endings and the
  break ending's tides stay as they are; tides are breach rifts too.
- **Defenses research** (the `def` branch, read only by the TD today)
  becomes Wardcraft: +1 warning day, smaller losses on a failed defense,
  weaker foes in breach rifts.
- Subclass gates `riftbreak_rank: "S"` (Rift-Breaker, Sainted Ember) mean
  "hold a breach rift of Rank S".
- DefenseRun/DefenseView stay in the repo, unreachable (same flag pattern).
- Save: `riftbreak_best` keeps its meaning (best rank held).

## Phase 2: camp events (0.64)

Hooked into `pass_time()` beside rivals, recruits, payday and hero requests.

### Rules
1. At most one event a day, on ~35% of days. Act I (until the first seal):
   opportunities only.
2. Every event is a decision, never a bare dice roll.
3. **Threats are real but fair:** announced the day before (calendar +
   `day_preview`), and always with ways to soften them: pay, assign a hero
   (busy that day, like training), or take the loss. Losses can hurt
   (a damaged building, 15-25% of spare Gold, a wounded hero, morale).
4. Events are mid/late-game sinks: they spend Gold and Essence on things
   that matter.
5. Per-event cooldowns, weights by act and guild state; the campaign sim
   picks options automatically so tuning stays measurable.

## Phase 3: lane map (0.65)

Today: each middle floor offers 2 unconnected picks. New: a connected map
(Slay the Spire style), so a route can be planned.
- **Width grows faster than length:** 3 lanes (Lesser, F-E), 4 (D-B),
  5 (A and up). Floors: as today, +1 at A, +2 at S and above.
- Each node links to 1-2 nodes in adjacent lanes on the next floor; every
  route has a campfire before the boss and at least one reachable elite;
  treasure more often sits behind an elite. Side branches: a detour with an
  extra elite to a cache, rejoining the main path.
- Hidden nodes at S and above, revealed by scouting (a Pathfinder or Stalker
  in the party, or a consumable).
- New nodes using existing systems: **Anvil** (a free Forge level),
  **Shrine of a Path** (XP or a temporary Twist for a hero on that Path),
  **Trainer's echo** (an XP boost).
- UI: a vertical, scrollable node map that fits the phone layout.
- Old in-flight runs finish on the old floor format.
- After it lands: re-run the readout calibration and the economy sims
  (more nodes = more rewards).

## Built (0.63.0, 2026-10-08)

All three phases shipped together. Differences from the plan above:
- "Two heroes quarrel" was dropped from the event list: hero requests
  already have a feud. 15 events, not 16.
- Breach rifts keep their per-fight loot; holding pays through the old
  `resolve_breach` (+Quartermaster). The day passes once (finish_run).
- The breach flag, the Descent depth and the Charter War's Morrow were not
  saved with a run; they are now (a reloaded Descent used to forget its depth).
- Lane map: the floor before the boss is always a lone campfire (every route
  passes it). Side-branch detours were left out; crossing lanes already give
  the route choice. New nodes: anvil, shrine, echo (LANE_EXTRAS).
- Scouting is a Trapper or a Stalker (by Path) in the party.
- On phones the run bar drops its floor pips outside fights (the map shows
  the floors) so the row fits.
- Tuned with campaign_sim (4 guilds x 2 profiles x 45 days). The first build
  stalled progress (casual: no Act V; investor: no Act VI). Causes and fixes:
  - Longer top ranks (+1 A, +2 S+) cost boss fights against a readout
    calibrated for the old length: RANK_EXTRA_FLOORS is empty for now
    (width only). Lengthen again only with a recalibrated readout.
  - Lane nodes had ~1.5 ways on (the old forks had 2): every node now leads
    to its own lane and one or both neighbours; at most one hazard and one
    elite per floor, so a route can always go around them.
  - Threats compounded: weights 2 -> 1, fever lays up one hero (was two),
    bandits take 15% (was 25%), the tremor 5% Essence (was 10%).
  - The sim never repaired damaged buildings; it does now (players see the
    status line). It also turns camp events off with camp=0.
  - After tuning: casual Act V done 4/4 (0.62: 2/4), Act VI 2/4 (2/4);
    investor Act VI done 2/4, all four in Act VI (0.62: 3/4 done).
- A party knocked out by a hazard before a fight could only leave by the
  run bar's Retreat; the fight screen now says so and offers to leave.
