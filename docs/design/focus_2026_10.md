# Focus, October 2026: fewer things at once, one system per job

Decided with the user on 2026-10-09, after the four-seat review of 0.64.1
("too many systems for a new player"). **The heart of Guildhold is raising
heroes, running the guild and the rift run**; the story supports them.
Nothing is cut: systems that don't belong in the first hours are revealed
later, and systems that do the same job are merged.

Two releases:
- **0.65 (before the playtest):** Part A, the reveal schedule. Part B, the
  Accord Hall, which merges the two upgrade trees and adds Path Mastery as an
  Essence sink.
- **0.66 (straight after):** Part C, gear. One Smithy, relics that bend
  rules, and Path relics carried by heroes.

## Why

| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Almost everything opens in the first 4-7 days | Quests at 1 seal, Management 2, Rival 3, Crafting 5, Daily 7, the Tower and Champions at the end of Act I. The sims finish Act I on day 4 (FEATURE_UNLOCKS, sim064b). |
| Two upgrade trees sell the same goods for different money | Four of the six Hall Works wings repeat a Management node: War Room = Drill Yard (damage/HP), Library = Barracks Lv5 (XP), Healers' Wing = Infirmary (recovery), Reliquary = Relic Vault (slots). |
| About 10 ways to raise gear power, each worth a few percent | Items: drops, attunement, crafting, the Forge, reforge, salvage. Rolled relics: specials and triggers, levels, rerolls, awakening, crafting. Plus 54 rule relics. |
| Path relics are collected, not chosen | About 23 of 30 per guild in 45 days, for 3-6 slots (sim064b). |
| Essence piles up while Gold runs short in mid-game | Casual guilds bank 4-11k Essence by day 45. Gold has no sink between "hall built" and the postgame (07_economy_sinks.md). |

## Principles
1. **One new thing at a time**, each brought in by a person or a story moment, never by a bare tab appearing.
2. **One system per job.** If two systems give the same kind of bonus, merge them.
3. **Gold builds, Essence empowers.** Buildings cost Gold. Heroes, Paths, relics and champions cost Essence.
4. **Old saves keep working.** Levels carry over, spent currency stays spent, and anything removed is refunded.

### How it works
- `FEATURE_UNLOCKS` (GameDataArt) gets an `"act"` gate beside the seal count. The new features are training_yard, hall_rooms_*, smithy, smithy_full, relics, rival, wardcraft, descent, daily, tower and wings.
  - `feature_unlocked` reads the gate.
  - A hidden feature's hotspot, tab and Rift Hall gate don't render at all; today they are greyed out.
- Every unlock arrives as a short **story card from a character**, using the existing `pending_stories` and voice lines where they exist. Wen introduces the hall rooms, Dobbs the Smithy, a Path trainer the first Path. Text only where there's no voice line.
- **Returning players:** if a past guild reached Act II or later, everything up to Act II opens from day 1, and the cards are skipped (a toast instead).
- **Saves:** a guild already past a gate keeps what it has (`features_seen` is set on load, as now).
- **Sim:** campaign_sim already uses the gates. The haul and Resolve moving to Act II needs one sim pass. Targets: Act timings unchanged, and Resolve Wavering on 5-15% of boss doors.

## Part B: the Accord Hall (0.65)

### Rooms (the Management tree, renamed and re-priced)
- The 13 Management nodes become the **rooms of the Accord Hall**, keeping their levels, perks and orders: Barracks, Infirmary, Drill Yard, Essence Amplifiers, Wardstones, Trade Network, Scouts' Lodge, Armory, Quartermaster, Palisade, Watchtower, Relic Vault, Arcane Lab.
- **Cost: Gold, 80 + 80 × level** (1,200 a room, 15,600 for all 13). Today they cost Essence, 50 + 50 × level, except Wardcraft. Upkeep is unchanged (12 Gold a week per level).
- The rooms open in the stages of Part A.
- **The hall's art** follows the guild tier: total room levels of 10 / 25 / 40 move the scaffolded ruin to rebuilding to restored. Today the art follows the number of wings.

### Wings (Hall Works, now a choice)
- At the **end of Act I, Act III and Act V** a card offers **two of the remaining wings**, and the player builds one.
- Cost: 800 / 1,400 / 2,000 Gold. A guild ends up with 3 of the 6 wings: its identity.
- Each wing's effect is rewritten so that **no wing repeats a room**:

| Wing | Identity | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Chapel | Sustain | Standing heroes mend 6% after every won fight; campfires restore +1 Resolve |
| Smithy | Gear | Tempering costs 25% less; combining keeps the best stat line of the three |
| Library | Learning | Shrines and trainer's echoes give double |
| War Room | Fighting | Elites pay +50%; one free Rally order per rift |
| Healers' Wing | Care | Injuries never leave scars; a downed hero recovers in one day |
| Reliquary | Relics | +1 relic slot; Path relic offers show 4, not 3 |

- **Saves:** wings already built stay, effects as rewritten. A guild that already has 3 or more is offered no more.

### Path Mastery (the new Essence sink)
Rooms leaving Essence (and, in 0.66, relic levels) make the Essence surplus worse. Essence needs a repeatable sink that feels like progress and belongs to the heart of the game: the heroes.
- Once a hero has finished their Path (stage 3), they can train **Mastery ranks** at the Training Yard, ranks 1-10.
  - Each rank makes their Path rule's numbers 4% stronger: Kindling Heat, Guardian share, Execute bonus and so on.
  - Cost: **100 × rank Essence** (5,500 per hero for all 10), plus one yard day.
- **Where it goes:** a `mastery` int on Hero. The Path rules in Combat already use constants (BLOODRAGE_CAP, COMBO_STEP, ...). Each read site multiplies by `1 + 0.04 × mastery` through one helper, `_mx(h)`.
- **Sim:** Essence at day 45 should fall from 4-11k to 1-4k without changing act timings. The per-Path report (review item) runs alongside.

## Part C: gear (0.66)

### C1. One Smithy
- The Forge and Crafting become **one screen with four actions**. The building in the hamlet is the Smithy, the hotspot that is the Forge today.
  - **Temper:** today's Forge.
  - **Reforge:** a stat line.
  - **Combine:** 3 items of a rarity become 1 of the next rarity.
  - **Salvage.**
- **Combining relics goes away** (see C2).
- The Crafting tab and hotspot disappear; the Smithy tab replaces both.

### C2. Items carry stats, relics bend rules
- **The relic pool becomes rule-benders only:** 14 uniques, 10 Tower relics, 2 Endless relics and 30 Path relics, 56 in all.
- **Retired:** rolled relics (`gen_relic` with rolled specials and triggers) and relic levels, rerolls, awakening, scrapping, combining and the relic ward (`relic.hp`, the run's hazard shield). Item stats and Wardstones carry that job.
- **Where relics come from now:**

| Today (rolled relic) | 0.66 |
|---|---|
| Loot drops (`gen_loot`, relic share) | Items only. An elite's loot has a 10% chance of a unique relic not yet owned |
| Rift shop relic; Trade Lv5 "an Epic relic in every shop" | The shop sometimes sells a unique (price 3× an epic item); Trade Lv5 makes it every shop |
| Events and camp "relic" rewards, the peddler, the smugglers | A unique if any are left, else an epic item |
| Lore fragments that "ride on a relic" (GameStateLore:207) | They ride on an item (a keepsake) |
| Founding and legacy gifts ("rare relic") | "A unique relic" |
| The starting relic choice before a run (Relic Vault) | Removed. The Relic Vault becomes "+1 relic slot at Lv1, Lv3 and Lv5" |
| Arcane Lab "+5% to relic effects", relic upgrade discount | Becomes "treatments and respecs −10% a level; salvage returns +10% a level" |

- **Old saves:** every rolled relic turns into Essence (twice its salvage value), with one toast. Uniques, Tower and Path relics stay.

### C3. Path relics are carried by heroes
- A hero carries **at most one** Path relic, and only one of their own Path.
  - Picking an offer asks which hero of that Path takes it.
  - If no hero has that Path yet, it waits in the guild's chest until one does.
  - At camp, it can be passed between heroes of the same Path.
- It works when its carrier is in the party. `_pp_start` reads `pmods` from the party's carriers instead of the guild's equipped relics.
- Guild relic slots (3-6) now hold only uniques and Tower relics.
- **The offer:** relics for the roster's Paths only (two per Path, so up to 30 offers). Taking one of a Path's twins doesn't remove the other, since two heroes of a Path can each carry one. Declining pays the seal's full Essence, not half.
- **Saves:** Path relics in guild slots move to the strongest hero of their Path, or to the chest.

## Built (0.66)
- **One Smithy:** the Crafting screen became the Smithy (Inventory > Smithy, and the Arcane Lab's hub entry): temper the lineup's gear, combine three spare items (relics are never combined), reforge a spare item (its card), salvage. Tempering and salvage open with the Smithy reveal (Act II); combine and reforge with the apprentice (Act III). Salvage no longer needs the Arcane Lab.
- **Relics are rules:** `gen_loot` gives items only; `unique_or_epic()` is every relic reward (an unowned unique, else an epic item): finales, events, camp rewards, the peddler, legacy gifts; an elite's loot holds one 10% of the time (`ELITE_UNIQUE_CHANCE`); a rift shop sells one 20% of the time, every shop at Trade Lv5 (`SHOP_UNIQUE_CHANCE`, 3x an epic item's price). Retired: `gen_relic` and its rolls, relic levels, rerolls, awakening, scrapping, combining, the relic ward and relic damage, the starting relic choice, the Lab's +5% relic effects. Story fragments ride on keepsake items (`Item.lore`). The veteran start gives two rare items instead of two rolled relics.
- **Rooms:** the Relic Vault is a slot at Lv1/3/5; the Arcane Lab is treatments and respecs -10% a level and salvage +10% a level (treatments still need Lv1).
- **Path relics on heroes:** `Hero.path_relic`, `path_relic_chest`; `give_path_relic` (a chosen bearer, else the strongest without one, else the chest), `carry_path_relic` (the hero's Path tab), `set_down_path_relic` (leaving, a Path change, the chest button). The offer shows relics for the Paths the guild's heroes walk, party first; when more than one hero could carry it, the card asks who. Declining pays the seal's full Essence. `_pp_start` and `path_relic_value` read the carriers in the party.
- **Saves (v6):** `_convert_relics_066`: rolled relics become 2x their salvage Essence, every relic level's Essence comes back, Path relics go to the strongest hero of their Path (or the chest); one toast.
- Not built: a per-Path relic power report in the sim (the campaign sim still counts relics taken).
